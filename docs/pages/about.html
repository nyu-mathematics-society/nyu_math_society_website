Who we are

The NYU Mathematics Society is the student organization representing the Math Department at Courant and its students – we cater to all math-interested students, regardless of school, and function as the central social hub for these students to mingle and network. The club’s primary function is to act as an all-inclusive and welcoming community for math-oriented students and to facilitate social and academic events and talks.
