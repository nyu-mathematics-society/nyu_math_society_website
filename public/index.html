Welcome to the NYU Math Society

We’re crazy about math! We strive to learn more about the subject and its applications outside of the classroom through social events as well as lectures and sharing of research, often by NYU Courant’s own established professors. We’re not strictly an academic club so math majors and non-majors alike are all welcome. Drop in to chill, mingle and make friends, all in the spirit of learning!
